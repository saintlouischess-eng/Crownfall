# Crownfall credits

Original character designs, medieval-inspired heraldry, hall art, user interface, animation choreography and music compositions were created for Crownfall with AI-assisted development. Blender was used for the cavalry asset. The hall and textures combine procedural artwork and generated imagery; the palace-style painted ceiling is an original fantasy addition to a medieval-inspired setting.

The soundtrack contains twenty original compositions and arrangements performed with licensed Spitfire Symphony Orchestra Discover instruments through Kontakt 8 and Waveform Free 14. Players receive the finished recordings. No sample libraries are distributed, and players do not need music-production software.

Recorded combat and movement effects draw on CC0 collections by Ben Jaszczak and Brian Nelson / Still North Media and Kenney. Full attribution is in the game's `licenses/Recorded-Foley.md` and `licenses/Kenney-Impact-Sounds.txt`.

The game includes third-party components: three.js (MIT), chess.js (BSD-2-Clause), Microsoft WebView2 SDK, and .NET / Windows Desktop runtime components. Their license texts and notices accompany the Windows download. External UCI engines and opening books are not bundled and retain their own licenses.

This is a finished-game distribution. The original Crownfall source project, editable art projects, music-production projects and development tools remain private. No open-source license is granted for the original game; included third-party licenses remain in force.
